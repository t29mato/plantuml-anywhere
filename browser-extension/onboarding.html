PlantUML Anywhere — one more step

This extension previews local .puml / .plantuml files the moment you open them with file://. Browsers block extensions from accessing file:// pages by default, so you need to turn this on manually — the extension can't enable it for you.

Skip this step and the preview simply won't appear when you open a .puml file.
  1. Open chrome://extensions (brave://extensions on Brave)
  2. Click "Details" on the PlantUML Anywhere card
  3. Turn on "Allow access to file URLs"

Once that's set, open (or drag and drop) any local .puml file in your browser and it renders in place.
